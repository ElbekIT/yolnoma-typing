Section 01

Introduction & Scope

Welcome to the official Privacy Policy for Algorix (hereinafter referred to as the "Application" or "App"). The Application is an intuitive, modern, high-precision mobile calculator built using Google's Flutter framework, proudly developed and maintained by the YOLNOMA Team ("we", "our", or "us").

We are firmly committed to protecting the privacy of everyone who installs and uses Algorix. This document explains in clear, transparent, and binding terms our data handling practices. By installing and using Algorix, you acknowledge and agree to the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy.

Key Principle: Algorix is designed as a pure utility. It does not require user accounts, logins, cloud synchronization, or telemetry to fulfill its core functionality.
Section 02

Information We Do Not Collect (Zero Data Collection)

Unlike many mobile utilities, Algorix does NOT collect, harvest, store, transmit, or sell any personal or non-personal user data. Specifically, the Application does NOT gather any of the following:

  • Personally Identifiable Information (PII): We do not ask for or collect names, home addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, usernames, passwords, or government identification numbers.
  • Location Data: We do not access, track, or record your precise or approximate geographical coordinates (GPS, cellular tower triangulation, or Wi-Fi location).
  • User Files & Device Storage: We do not read, scan, upload, or access any documents, photos, videos, audio recordings, or downloads saved on your physical device.
  • Contacts & Communications: We do not access your address book, contact list, text messages, phone logs, or messaging history.
  • Biometric Data: No fingerprint, facial recognition, or iris scanning data is ever captured, requested, or processed by the Application.
  • Device Identifiers & Fingerprinting: We do not collect hardware identifiers such as IMEI numbers, MAC addresses, Android Advertising IDs (AAID), or hardware serial numbers for tracking or profiling purposes.
Zero Server Ingestion: Algorix maintains no external cloud databases or intake endpoints. There is no remote repository where your interactions could be stored.
Section 03

Local On-Device Computation & Offline Architecture

Every mathematical operation performed within Algorix—including standard arithmetic, scientific functions, trigonometry, percentage algorithms, memory registers (M+, M-, MR), and calculation history—is executed exclusively within your device's local memory (RAM) and application sandbox.

  • 100% Offline Functionality: Algorix is an offline-first application. It requires zero network connectivity to execute calculations. You can freely use Algorix while completely offline or in Airplane Mode.
  • Ephemeral & Local Storage: If you choose to save custom calculation history or interface preferences (such as light/dark mode theme or decimal precision), this information is written strictly to Android's private, encrypted-by-default app-specific storage. It is never transmitted across the internet.
  • Isolated Sandboxing: In full alignment with modern Android operating system security standards, Algorix runs in an isolated process sandbox, preventing any third-party app from intercepting your keystrokes or numerical outputs.
Section 04

Third-Party Services, SDKs & Tracking Technologies

To provide an untainted, uninterrupted, and privacy-respecting user experience, Algorix strictly adheres to a zero-tracking policy:

  • No Analytics SDKs: Algorix does not embed Google Analytics for Firebase, Flurry, Mixpanel, AppsFlyer, Amplitude, or any other diagnostic telemetry SDKs that monitor user behavior.
  • No Advertising Networks: We do not include third-party ad frameworks such as Google AdMob, Unity Ads, AppLovin, ironSource, or Meta Audience Network. You will not see intrusive banners, interstitial videos, or targeted advertisements inside Algorix.
  • No Tracking Cookies or Fingerprinting: The Application does not generate, store, or communicate tracking cookies, web beacons, or unique device fingerprints across sessions or applications.
Section 05

Section 06

Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Protecting the privacy of children is of paramount importance to the YOLNOMA Team. Algorix is designed as a general educational and everyday calculation tool suitable for users of all ages, including students and minors.

  • Full COPPA Compliance: In accordance with the United States Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), we do not knowingly request, collect, maintain, or disclose personal information from children under 13 years of age.
  • Full GDPR-K Compliance: In compliance with the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Article 8 regarding minors, we do not process personal data of children under 16 years of age.
  • Google Play Families Policy: Algorix complies in full with Google Play's Families Policy requirements. Because zero personal data is collected or transmitted, parents and educators can rest assured that children's online privacy and safety are completely uncompromised.
Section 07

Data Retention, User Rights & Deletion Guarantee

Because Algorix collects zero personal data and maintains no remote servers or centralized accounts:

  • No Cloud Records: There are no server-side records, databases, user profiles, or cloud backups to delete, modify, or export.
  • Immediate Local Purging: Any ephemeral calculation history or preferences retained on your local device can be deleted at any moment:
    • By tapping the "Clear History" button inside the Application's interface.
    • By going to your device's Settings → Apps → Algorix → Storage → Clear Data.
    • By simply uninstalling the Application from your device, which automatically causes the operating system to permanently wipe all private sandbox files.
  • Global Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA): Under international frameworks including GDPR and CCPA, users possess rights to access, rectify, and erase personal information. Because we retain zero personal data, your right to privacy is intrinsically guaranteed by the application's offline architecture.
Section 08

Application Security & Integrity

The security of your device is a top priority. Algorix is compiled using verified, production-grade Flutter toolchains with strict code optimization and zero unnecessary external dependencies. By removing the need for remote network communications, the Application eliminates attack surfaces associated with man-in-the-middle (MITM) interceptions, unencrypted network transit, or remote data breach vulnerabilities.
